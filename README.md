Privacy Policy for Soundless
Effective Date: Nov 13, 2026
Soundless ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Soundless mobile application.
1.
Information Collection and Use
Soundless does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information, user data, analytics, crash logs, or device identifiers. All custom sound mixes and user preferences are stored strictly and securely local on your device.
2.
Device Permissions
Soundless does not request or require any dangerous device permissions. It does not access or record microphone input, camera, location, contacts, external storage, or phone state.
3.
Offline Operation & Internet Access
Soundless is engineered to work 100% offline. It does not require an internet connection for core playback. Any optional online sound packs in future updates will be clearly labeled and are entirely optional.
4.
Third-Party Services & Ads
Soundless contains no advertisements, analytics SDKs, tracking libraries, or third-party data collection services.
5.
Children’s Privacy
Because Soundless does not collect any personal data of any kind, it is fully safe and compliant for users of all ages.
6.
Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within app store listings or documentation.
7.
Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out at our developer page. 
Thank you for your attention!
